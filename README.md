## 🎯 About Stutify

Stutify helps people who stutter gain confidence in their daily speech. The software tracks patterns in your speech and provides real-time feedback. It uses audio analysis to detect blocks or repetitions. It then suggests pacing adjustments to help you speak with more flow. You see clear visual cues on your screen that guide your breathing and rate of speech. This tool operates quietly in the background while you practice reading, talking, or presenting.

## 🛠️ Configuration

Upon launching Stutify for the first time, the program asks for permission to access your microphone. Click Allow to proceed. The software cannot function without audio input. 

Select your preferred microphone from the drop-down menu inside the settings window. Run the calibration test to ensure the software hears your voice clearly. The bars should move when you speak. If you see no movement, check your Windows sound settings to ensure your input device works.

## 📈 Key Features

- Real-time Speech Tracking: The software creates a visual graph of your speech timing. You see long pauses or sudden accelerations in real-time.
- Visual Pacing Guide: A small, unobtrusive bar on the side of your screen moves at a steady rate. Use this as a tempo guide to keep your speech rhythmic.
- Progress Reporting: Access the history tab to see how your fluency improves over weeks or months. The app charts your improvements to keep you motivated.
- Customizable Difficulty: Beginners can start with simple reading passages. Advanced users might adjust the sensitivity to detect subtle blocks during casual conversation.

## 💡 Usage Tips

Practice in short sessions of ten to fifteen minutes. Consistency builds better habits than long, sporadic sessions. Find a quiet room to reduce background noise, which helps the software identify your voice more accurately. Focus on your breathing rather than the visual feedback itself. The screen cues exist as a support tool, not as a source of pressure. 

If the software stops detecting your voice, stop for a moment and check that your microphone is still selected. Restarting the app often fixes minor connection errors.

## 🛡️ Troubleshooting

The application might trigger a security prompt during the first launch. This is normal for programs downloaded from the internet. Click More Info and then Run Anyway.

If the app feels slow, close other programs that use audio, such as video conference platforms or media players. This leaves more computer resources for the speech analysis engine to process your voice without lags.

Check for updates within the Help menu periodically. We release updates to fix bugs and improve the accuracy of our speech analysis. If you encounter a crash, please check the log file located in your installation folder. This file contains information that helps developers understand what went wrong on your computer.
